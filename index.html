Chong Tang

PhD Candidate in Leisure Management

Macau University of Science and Technology

My research examines how artificial intelligence and digital information environments shape employee and consumer behavior in hospitality and tourism. My current work spans human–AI collaboration and digital work, online reviews and user-generated content, and knowledge management, using both survey-based and computational approaches.

ORCID

Research

Human–AI Interaction & Digital Work

I study how employees interact and collaborate with artificial intelligence in contemporary service work, with particular attention to the cognitive and behavioral consequences of frequent AI use, human–AI collaboration, and technology-enabled work demands.

Online Reviews, UGC & Digital Consumer Behavior

My research examines how consumers interpret and respond to online reviews, user-generated content, and AI-assisted information in digital service environments. I am particularly interested in information overload, online decision-making, recommendation systems, and behavioral patterns embedded in large-scale platform data.

Knowledge Management & Organizational Learning

I am interested in how artificial intelligence changes the creation, transformation, sharing, and application of knowledge within organizations, including human–AI knowledge collaboration, organizational learning, employee development, and AI-supported training.

Methods: Structural equation modeling (SEM), survey research, large-scale online review analytics, computational text analysis, and LLM-assisted coding.
